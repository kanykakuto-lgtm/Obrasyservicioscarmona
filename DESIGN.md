## Brand & Style

The brand identity combines the precision of high-end architectural craft with the reliability of a heritage builder operating since 1995 across Toledo and South Madrid. The visual tone departs from chaotic commercial contracting and aligns instead with the disciplined clarity of an architectural studio: curated, structural, honest, and reassuringly solid.

### Core Character & Mood
- **Architectural Discipline:** Structured composition, geometric balance, clean spatial rhythms, and uncompromising alignment.
- **Mastery & Provenance:** Communicates nearly three decades of construction expertise without visual clutter or rustic clichés; it feels established yet completely contemporary.
- **Tactile Honesty:** Warm terracotta and earthy tones balance technical charcoals and crisp whites, evoking raw materials like fired brick, limestone, and warm oak.

### Design Movement
**Architectural Warm Modernism:** Minimalist foundational structure enriched with tactile material warmth, generous negative space, refined hairline borders, and subtle physical elevations.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around deep mineral charcoal anchors and architectural stone surfaces, punctuated by warm clay and terracotta accents that evoke construction heritage and interior finishes.

### Roles & Values
- **Primary (`#1E2229` - Deep Anthracite):** Dominant structural color used for primary navigation, high-emphasis headings, solid master buttons, and grounding container sections.
- **Secondary (`#C25E38` - Rich Terracotta):** Strategic accent used for primary interactive highlights, badges, conversion accents, and selective metric markers.
- **Tertiary (`#D97448` - Warm Ochre-Amber):** Supporting interactive color used for hover transitions, subtle focus indicators, and gradient touches on editorial badges.
- **Neutral Stone Palette:**
  - `Surface Base`: `#FFFFFF` (Pure pristine white)
  - `Surface Subdued`: `#F8F9FA` (Soft limestone white for alternate page bands and backdrop cards)
  - `Border / Hairline`: `#ECEEF1` (Muted mortar grey for structural outlines and dividers)
  - `Text Muted`: `#5C6470` (Slate charcoal for secondary body text and metadata)
  - `Text Body`: `#2D323B` (Readable off-black for high legibility without pure black harshness)

### Application Rules
- Maintain an 80/15/5 ratio: 80% white, cream, and soft grey; 15% deep anthracite; 5% terracotta focus elements.
- Never use `#C25E38` for large background washes; reserve it exclusively for focal CTAs, selective line tags, and precise micro-accents.

## Typography

The pairing combines the geometric, grounded architecture of **Manrope** for expressive headlines with the warm, open humanist cadence of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for interfaces and narrative passages.

- **Headlines (`Manrope`):** Distinctive alternate forms, tight tracking on large scales, and balanced geometric shapes reflect structural engineering and contemporary blueprint aesthetics.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Generous x-height and clear terminals yield effortless legibility on technical specification cards, project descriptions, and quotation forms.
- **Pre-headers & Badges:** `label-caps` must always be rendered in uppercase with wide letter spacing (`0.12em`) to introduce architectural case studies and section themes with editorial authority.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 12-column responsive layout system provides deliberate compositional structure. Spacing is treated as a physical material—ample, unhurried negative space establishes a sense of quality and transparency.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `1240px` max-width container, `32px` (`2rem`) gutters, dynamic margins (`min(4vw, 48px)`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, full-width with `24px` (`1.5rem`) side gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `16px` (`1rem`) side padding, single column vertical stacks for architectural deliverables.

### Vertical Cadence
- Major homepage sections employ `space-4xl` (`6rem`) top/bottom padding to let imagery and project statistics breathe.
- Component card interiors maintain generous spacing: `space-xl` (`2rem`) on desktop, scaling down to `space-lg` (`1.5rem`) on mobile.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses subtle ambient shadows, architectural plane layering, and hairline borders rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Layer (Ground):** `#FFFFFF` as the main canvas or `#F8F9FA` for dedicated secondary full-bleed segments (e.g., project showcases, client credentials).
- **Elevated Surfaces:** Rendered in pure `#FFFFFF` over `#F8F9FA` backgrounds, separated by crisp `1px` borders in `#ECEEF1`.
- **Contrast Surfaces:** Deep `#1E2229` tone reserved for the master footer, hero banner accents, and high-impact contact blocks.

### Shadow Scale
- **Flat Border Tier (Default Cards):** `0 0 0 1px #ECEEF1` (No blur shadow, purely structural).
- **Subtle Ambient (Resting Cards):** `0 1px 3px rgba(30, 34, 41, 0.04), 0 6px 16px rgba(30, 34, 41, 0.03)`.
- **Hover Lift (Interactive Cards):** `0 8px 24px rgba(30, 34, 41, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(30, 34, 41, 0.04)` combined with a subtle `-2px` Y-axis translation.
- **Floating Modals & Flyouts:** `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(30, 34, 41, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The shape system embraces **Level 1 (Soft)** roundedness. It prioritizes straight architectural lines, crisp geometry, and subtly softened corners (`4px` to `8px`) that feel polished and technical without looking playful or bulbous.

### Token Mapping
- **Input Fields & Buttons:** `4px` (`0.25rem`) border-radius for an engineering-inspired silhouette.
- **Feature Cards & Project Tiles:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) border-radius for a composed balance of structure and modern framing.
- **Tags, Chips & Badges:** `4px` (`0.25rem`) border-radius (retaining crisp rectangular proportions rather than stadium pills).
- **Photography & Project Galleries:** Strictly `8px` (`0.5rem`) radius with optional `1px` inner boundary strokes to preserve image edge integrity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Anthracite):** Solid `#1E2229` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` border radius, `12px 24px` padding, font weight 600 (`label-ui`). Hover shifts to `#2D323B` with subtle scale transition.
- **Terracotta Accent (Conversion Focus):** Solid `#C25E38` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Used for main estimation actions (e.g., "Solicitar Presupuesto"). Hover transitions to `#D97448`.
- **Secondary (Outlined):** `1px` solid `#1E2229` or `#ECEEF1`, transparent background, `#1E2229` text. Hover fills with `#F8F9FA`.

### Cards & Project Galleries
- **Project Showcase Card:** White background with a `1px` border in `#ECEEF1`, containing a 16:10 ratio photograph with an `8px` top radius. Bottom container uses `space-lg` internal padding, featuring location tags (`Yuncos`, `Toledo`, `Madrid Sur`), project completion year, and clear title typography.
- **Service Offering Card:** White surface, subtle ambient shadow, with an earthy accent indicator (`#C25E38`) appearing on hover alongside the title.

### Form Inputs & Quotation Selectors
- **Text & Select Fields:** `44px` height, `1px` solid border in `#ECEEF1`, `#FFFFFF` surface, `12px` horizontal padding. On focus: active border shifts to `#C25E38` with `0 0 0 3px rgba(194, 94, 56, 0.15)`.
- **Radio Buttons & Service Checkers:** Custom square-soft selector boxes (`4px` radius) styled with deep anthracite borders, filling with `#C25E38` upon selection.

### Chips & Badges
- **Experience / Heritage Badge:** Uppercase `label-caps`, background `#F8F9FA`, text `#1E2229`, bordered by `#ECEEF1`, displaying milestone content such as `"DESDE 1995 · TOLEDO & MADRID"`.
- **Status Indicator:** Tiny inline circular dot (`#C25E38`) accompanied by neutral slate text for live project tracking or availability alerts.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Before / After Comparison Slider:** Clean hairline vertical divider in white with a textured terracotta thumb, allowing clients to inspect structural transformations.
- **Interactive Budget Calculator:** Multi-step card container with subtle progress hairpins, allowing users to specify square meters, scope (Integral, Cocina, Baño, Fachada), and receive estimated turnaround schedules.
